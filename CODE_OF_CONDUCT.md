# Community guidelines

Moagram welcomes constructive participation from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

- Discuss ideas and code respectfully; make criticism specific and useful.
- Help others reproduce problems and understand the project.
- Respect privacy: do not share personal information, private messages, credentials, or account/session data without permission.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, spam, or make personal attacks.
- Keep issues and pull requests relevant to the project.

The repository maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive threads, or restrict participation. Use GitHub's report or block controls for abuse. Do not repeat private or abusive material in a public issue. Security concerns should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

These guidelines apply to this repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions.
